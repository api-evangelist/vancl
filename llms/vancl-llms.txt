# VANCL (凡客诚品)

> VANCL (凡客诚品) is a Chinese direct-to-consumer online apparel brand founded in October 2007, selling its own-brand men's wear, women's wear, shoes, children's clothing, and home goods through vancl.com and third-party storefronts on Tmall, JD.com, Douyin, and Youzan. VANCL publishes no public API, developer portal, or machine-readable discovery surface today; its former open-platform hosts (open.vancl.com, api.vancl.com) no longer respond.
